Town Job Math Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the developer of the app "Town Job Math" (Japanese title: まちかどバイト算数; "the App") handles information.

1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect any information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, location or device identifiers. The developer does not collect usage analytics or advertising data. The App contains no ads or analytics tools.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (the name you enter in the game, coins, shops passed, records of right and wrong answers, and so on) and your settings are stored on your device. Only when you choose to upload from the "iCloud" button on the title screen is your progress saved to your own iCloud (an Apple service). In either case, nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. Progress that hasn't been uploaded to iCloud is deleted when you delete the App.

3. In-app purchase

Buying and restoring "Full Town" is handled through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment information; the developer never receives it. Apple's handling of your information follows Apple's privacy policy.

4. Game Center

If your device is signed in to Game Center, the App sends the trophies you earn to Apple's Game Center. Apple manages this information, and the developer does not receive information that identifies you.

5. Network use

The App uses the network only for the Apple services in sections 2 (iCloud), 3 and 4, and to check for and download voice data for languages other than Japanese (Japanese voices are built into the App). Voice data is downloaded from GitHub Pages, the same host as this support page. As with any download, your device's IP address reaches GitHub, but the developer does not receive it or use it to identify you. GitHub handles this information under GitHub's privacy policy. The App does not otherwise connect to the internet or open outside websites.

6. Children

The App is designed for elementary school children. As described above, the developer does not collect personal information, so no information that requires parental consent is collected. Before the purchase screen, the App shows a notice asking the child to show it to a grown-up.

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
