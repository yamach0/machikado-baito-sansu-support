How to play

Each job is 10 problems and takes just a few minutes, so it's easy to fit into a busy day.

Pick a shop on the town map

Go into a shop with a "Hiring!" sign. Once you've passed a shop, you can take on its harder ★2 and ★3 jobs too.

Hear about the job from the owner

The animal shop owners explain what the job is about, with voice.

Answer the customers' questions

Get 8 out of 10 right to pass. Stuck? Tap "Help!" for an explanation with pictures.

Enjoy the town with your pay

Spend your coins on tools and decorations for the town. Each pass opens the next shop, all the way to the Airport.

FAQ

What age is it for?

The first shop starts with simple addition that a 6-year-old can do. Later shops cover fractions, decimals and speed, so it's best for ages 6 to 12 (elementary school).

Does it cost anything?

The download is free. The first 4 shops, from the Onigiri Shop to the Bakery, are free to play. The other 10 shops, from the Station to the Airport, open with a one-time purchase called "Full Town" (shops open one by one as you pass the shop before). There are no subscriptions and no ads.

Can my child buy it by accident?

The App Store asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your password before any purchase. With Family Sharing's "Ask to Buy", a child can't buy without a parent's approval. Before the purchase screen, the app asks the child to show it to a grown-up.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad. It also runs on Mac computers with Apple silicon. An Android version is in the works.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the English voices are downloaded. The app only goes online to download voices, to upload or download progress with iCloud, for purchases and for Game Center.

What happens to my purchase on a new device or after reinstalling?

With the same Apple Account, go to "My Record" on the town map, then "About Full Town", and tap "Restore purchase". You won't be charged again. "Full Town" also works with Family Sharing, so your family's devices can use it too.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Progress is saved on the device. If you upload it from the "iCloud" button at the top right of the title screen, you can download it on another device, or after reinstalling, and carry on (you need to be signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account). Progress that hasn't been uploaded is lost if you delete the app.

How can I see how my child is doing?

"My Record" on the town map shows how many shops have been passed, the number of right answers for each topic, and recent mistakes.

What are the trophies (Game Center)?

Trophies are awards you get for passing shops and more. They are recorded only when the device is signed in to Game Center. You can play the whole game without signing in.

Which languages are there?

English and Japanese. The app uses English unless the device is set to Japanese, and you can switch any time from "言語 / Language" on the title screen. English voices are downloaded the first time you use them; the Japanese voices are built in.
